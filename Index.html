About Us

Crafting Luxurious
Living Experiences

At Mourine Momo LUX Rug, we believe that every home deserves to be a sanctuary of beauty and comfort. Founded in the heart of Ago Palace, Lagos, we have spent over seven years curating the world's finest rugs, furniture, and home accessories.

Our collection spans from hand-knotted Persian masterpieces to contemporary African-inspired designs, paired with bespoke furniture that transforms spaces into personal galleries of art. We serve clients who understand that luxury is not just about price — it is about the story, the craftsmanship, and the emotion a piece brings to your home.
